Research Title

Integrated Vehicle Maintenance and Inventory Management System with Decision Support for JA Noble Enterprise Inc.

This capstone project develops a system that helps JA Noble Enterprise Inc. manage assets and inventory efficiently, with AI-powered decision support for stock replenishment and preventive maintenance scheduling.

Statement of the Problem

This study aimed to address the problems encountered by JA Noble Enterprise Inc. in managing vehicle maintenance and inventory through paper-based processes and disconnected spreadsheets. Specifically, it sought to answer the following questions:

  1. 1

    How can the existing paper-based processes and disconnected spreadsheets be integrated into a centralized system for managing vehicle maintenance and inventory?

  2. 2

    How can the system monitor vehicle service records, including the materials used for each maintenance job, and provide real-time visibility of inventory levels?

  3. 3

    How can the system help prevent stockouts of important parts through low-stock alerts and reorder recommendations?

  4. 4

    How can the system improve Preventive Maintenance Scheduling (PMS) through automated notifications of upcoming maintenance schedules?

  5. 5

    How can the system provide customers with secure access to their vehicles' service history and maintenance due dates?

  6. 6

    How can the system generate useful management reports to support decision-making and improve operational efficiency?

  7. 7

    How acceptable is the developed system to its intended users in terms of its functionality and usability?

Objectives

This study aimed to develop, test, and evaluate an Integrated Vehicle Maintenance and Inventory Management System with Decision Support for JA Noble Enterprise Inc., with regard to functional correctness using systematic testing and user acceptance through the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM). Specifically, this study aimed to:

Objective 1

Design and develop the integrated system

To design and develop a system of Integrated Vehicle Maintenance and Inventory Management System with Decision Support using web and mobile application for JA Noble Enterprise Inc. with the following functional components:

  1. Vehicle Maintenance Management module with digital work order creation, vehicle-linked service history tracking, and service status monitoring
  2. Mobile-Based Inventory Management module with QR code and barcode scanning for real-time stock monitoring and automated inventory updates
  3. Decision Support System (DSS) module that generates low-stock alerts, reorder recommendations for stock replenishment, and automated Preventive Maintenance Scheduling (PMS) notifications
  4. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) AI Assistant module that retrieves database records to generate conversational responses and automated management reports in PDF and Excel formats
  5. Secure mobile customer portal with role-based authentication for viewing vehicle service history and PMS due dates
Objective 2

Test the system’s major features

To test the functionalities of the developed system based on the major features:

  1. Vehicle Maintenance Management
  2. Mobile-Based Inventory Management with QR/Barcode Scanning
  3. Decision Support System (DSS) for stock replenishment and PMS scheduling
  4. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for AI assistants
Objective 3

Evaluate user acceptance and behavioral intention

To evaluate user acceptance and behavioral intention to use of the developed system:

  1. Quality Factors
  2. Perceived Ease of Use
  3. Attitude Toward Using
  4. Behavioral Intention to Use
  5. Experience

Methodology

The study employed a Developmental Research Design with a mixed-methods approach to develop and evaluate the Integrated Vehicle Maintenance and Inventory Management System with Decision Support for JA Noble Enterprise Inc. The researchers gathered data through interviews and on-site observations, developed the system using the Agile Scrum Framework, and conducted technical testing and user evaluation to assess its functionality, usability, and acceptability.

Development process

The system followed six stages of the Agile Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC):

  1. 1Requirement Planning

    Gathering operational requirements and identifying existing problems in vehicle maintenance and inventory management.

  2. 2Design

    Creating the system architecture, database structure, and user interface layouts.

  3. 3Development

    Building and integrating the web application, mobile application, DSS, and RAG-powered AI Assistant.

  4. 4Testing

    Conducting functional testing to verify system features and identify errors.

  5. 5Deployment and Feedback

    Implementing a pilot version and gathering feedback from intended users.

  6. 6Maintenance

    Improving system performance, fixing identified issues, and ensuring data consistency.

System testing and evaluation

Black-Box Testing10

IT experts evaluated the system’s functionality.

User evaluation144

respondents across three user groups.

  • 3 administrators
  • 16 staff members
  • 125 customers

Evaluation questionnaires were based on the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality standards and Technology Acceptance Model (TAM). The collected responses were analyzed using weighted mean and standard deviation to determine the system's overall acceptability.
